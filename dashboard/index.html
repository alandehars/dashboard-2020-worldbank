FULL-SAMPLE RESEARCH VERDICT

A broad contraction, with uneven evidence beyond it.

In the supplied selection, 45 of 55 countries with reported GDP growth contracted. GDP per capita and human capital move together by rank across 55 complete pairs (Spearman rho 0.879). The file cannot support a world estimate or causal claim because country selection and observation dates are undocumented.

RECOMMENDED USE
  1. Label each comparison with its observed country count and weighting.
  2. Favor well-covered measures for broad comparisons; inspect missingness before ranking countries.
  3. Confirm series IDs, extraction dates and country selection before trend or policy analysis.
